Answer: **What is Diabetes?**  
Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic disorder in which blood glucose (sugar) levels are elevated. This occurs because the body either does not produce enough insulin (as in Type 1 diabetes) or cannot use the insulin it makes effectively (as in Type 2 diabetes). Insulin, a hormone from the pancreas, is needed for glucose from food to enter cells for energy.

**Common Symptoms**  
The classic signs of high blood sugar (hyperglycemia) include:

- Frequent urination, especially at night (polyuria)  
- Excessive thirst (polydipsia)  
- Unexplained weight loss  
- Increased hunger (polyphagia)  
- Fatigue and weakness  
- Blurred vision  
- Slow‑healing cuts or bruises  
- Frequent infections (skin, gum, bladder, vaginal)  
- Tingling or numbness in the hands and feet (neuropathy)  
- Dry, itchy skin  

(Note: Some people with Type 2 diabetes may have no symptoms initially.)
