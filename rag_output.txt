Answer: **What is Diabetes?**  
Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic disorder in which blood glucose (sugar) levels are higher than normal. This occurs because the body either does not produce enough insulin (the hormone that helps glucose enter cells) or cannot use the insulin it makes effectively.

**Common Symptoms**  
- Frequent urination, especially at night (polyuria)  
- Excessive thirst (polydipsia)  
- Unexplained weight loss  
- Increased hunger (polyphagia)  
- Fatigue and weakness  
- Blurred vision  
- Slow‑healing cuts or bruises  
- Frequent infections (skin, gum, bladder, vaginal)  
- Tingling or numbness in the hands and feet (neuropathy)  
- Dry, itchy skin  

Some individuals, particularly those with early‑stage Type 2 diabetes, may have no noticeable symptoms at first. If you notice any of these signs, consider discussing them with a healthcare professional.
