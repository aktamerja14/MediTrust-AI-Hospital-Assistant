About MediTrust

Setting the Benchmark for Ethical, Patient-Centric Medicine

Founded with the belief that exceptional healthcare should be dignified, transparent, and universally accessible, MediTrust Hospital has grown into one of the country's most respected medical institutions.

Our campus integrates modern surgical suites, intensive trauma units, fully automated diagnostic laboratories, and dedicated recovery pavilions staffed by top board-certified practitioners.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, compassionate clinical care by integrating advanced medical science with human warmth and moral responsibility.

Our Vision

To be the preeminent regional and international medical sanctuary, pioneering breakthroughs in preventive care and patient safety.

Comprehensive Care

24/7 Clinical & Hospital Services

From life-saving acute trauma intervention to automated lab testing and round-the-clock pharmacy dispensing, our integrated services ensure seamless continuity of care.

Emergency & Trauma Care

Certified Level-1 Emergency Department operating 24 hours every day with dedicated resuscitation bays, cardiac resuscitation, and instant surgical triage.

  • Rapid ALS Ambulance Fleet Dispatch
  • On-call Multidisciplinary Trauma Surgeons
  • Direct Access to Critical Resuscitation

Automated Clinical Laboratory

CAP-accredited laboratory delivering precision diagnostic pathology, biochemistry, hematology, microbiology, and molecular genetics with rapid digital reporting.

  • Ultra-fast 2-hour Routine Blood Panel Reports
  • Sterile Home Sample Collection Available
  • Barcode-tracked Barcode Identification

24/7 In-House Pharmacy

Fully stocked pharmacy carrying verified pharmaceuticals, temperature-monitored biologics, oncology medicines, and surgical consumables overseen by licensed pharmacists.

  • Authentic 100% Sourced Medications
  • Digital E-Prescription Fulfillment
  • Medication Counseling & Dosage Guidance

Advanced Diagnostic Radiology

High-resolution imaging technology for early detection and precision treatment planning, managed by fellowship-trained radiologist consultants.

  • 3.0 Tesla MRI & 128-Slice Dual-Source CT
  • High-Resolution 4D Doppler Ultrasound
  • Low-Radiation Digital Mammography & Bone DEXA

Critical Care & Inpatient Suites

Modern intensive care units (ICU, CCU, NICU) paired with ergonomic private recovery rooms engineered to promote accelerated, infection-free healing.

  • 1:1 Patient-to-Nurse Critical Care Ratio
  • HEPA Filtered Positive Pressure Theaters
  • Dedicated Family Counseling Rooms

Virtual Telehealth Consults

Connect securely with our clinical specialists from anywhere in the world for secondary opinions, lab report reviews, and post-surgery checkups.

  • HIPAA-Compliant Encrypted Video Calls
  • Digital Prescription Delivery to App
  • Fast Appointment Slot Confirmations
